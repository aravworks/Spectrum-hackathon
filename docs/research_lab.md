# Pollution & Climate Coordination Lab / Research Data Lab

## Goal
Provide transparent, queryable, aggregated metrics for decision-making and research.

## Raw operational metrics
- reports/day
- estimated kg/day
- verified kg/day
- pickup completion rate
- missed pickup rate
- resolution time
- waste category distribution
- recyclable recovery
- hotspot recurrence
- route distance/time
- payment totals

## Environmental metrics
- estimated CO2e
- estimated methane potential
- avoided emissions scenarios
- waste diverted from disposal
- recycling/reuse rates

## Research datasets
Datasets should be:
- documented
- versioned
- anonymized
- access-controlled
- reproducible

Each dataset has:
- owner
- purpose
- schema
- refresh schedule
- methodology
- access level

## Exports
CSV/Parquet for authorized researchers.
Avoid exporting exact consumer addresses or personally identifying data.

## Dashboard layers
1. operational
2. waste composition
3. spatial
4. environmental
5. socioeconomic/contextual data if lawfully available
